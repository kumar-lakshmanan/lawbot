Here are 7 simple legal case examples, each falling under a different category of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), written in a simple style similar to your cheque-bounce example.

1. Cheating

Ramesh told Suresh that he could arrange a government job for ₹5 lakh.
 Trusting him, Suresh paid the money.
 After receiving the amount, Ramesh stopped answering calls.
 It was later discovered that he had no such contacts or authority.

2. Theft

Anita parked her bicycle outside a supermarket.
 When she returned after shopping, the bicycle was missing.
 CCTV footage showed a man taking it away without permission.
 The bicycle belonged to Anita and had not been sold or gifted.

3. Criminal Trespass

Kumar's neighbour entered his farmland without permission.
 Despite being warned several times, he continued entering the property.
 He also damaged a portion of the fence while entering.

4. Voluntarily Causing Hurt

During an argument over parking, Vijay hit Arun with a wooden stick.
 Arun suffered injuries on his arm and required medical treatment.
 Several people witnessed the incident.
 The attack was not accidental.

5. Criminal Intimidation

Priya complained about illegal dumping near her house.
 The person responsible called her and threatened serious consequences if she continued complaining.
 The threats caused Priya fear and distress.
 She recorded the call and approached the police.

6. Mischief (Damage to Property)

A shop owner had a dispute with a nearby vendor.
 Late at night, the vendor damaged the shop's signboard and windows.
 The damage was captured on a security camera.
 The owner suffered financial loss due to the incident.

7. House-Trespass and Theft

While a family was away on vacation, an unknown person entered their house through a rear window.
 The intruder stole jewellery and cash from a cupboard.
 Neighbours later noticed suspicious activity and informed the police.
 An investigation confirmed forced entry into the house.
