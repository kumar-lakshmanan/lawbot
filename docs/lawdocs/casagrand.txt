Casegrand International School (CIS) Student Conduct & Discipline Rules
CIS-S001 : Damage to Another Student's Property

Rule Description:
 Any student who intentionally damages, destroys, tears, breaks, writes on, or otherwise harms another student's personal property.

Penalty:

Student must replace the damaged item with a new equivalent item.
Fine of ₹100.
Mandatory parent meeting with class teacher.
Written warning recorded.
CIS-S002 : Repeated Damage to Property

Rule Description:
 A student who damages another student's property more than once during the academic year.

Penalty:

Replace damaged property.
Fine of ₹500.
Parent meeting.
Suspension for 3 school working days.
CIS-S003 : Failure to Carry School ID Card

Rule Description:
 Student enters school premises without official school ID card.

Penalty:

First occurrence: Verbal warning.
Second occurrence: Written warning.
Third occurrence: ₹100 fine.
Fourth occurrence onwards: Parent meeting mandatory.
CIS-S004 : Bullying or Harassment

Rule Description:
 Insulting, threatening, mocking, teasing, or harassing another student physically, verbally, or online.

Penalty:

Written warning.
Parent meeting.
School counselling session.
Suspension up to 5 working days for severe cases.
CIS-S005 : Physical Fighting

Rule Description:
 Engaging in physical fights, pushing, punching, kicking, or encouraging violence on campus.

Penalty:

Suspension of 3-7 working days.
Parent meeting.
Behaviour review by discipline committee.
CIS-S006 : Classroom Disruption

Rule Description:
 Intentionally disturbing class, shouting, throwing objects, or preventing teaching activities.

Penalty:

Written warning.
After three incidents: Parent meeting.
Community service within school.
CIS-S007 : Cheating During Examination

Rule Description:
 Copying answers, possessing unauthorized materials, or assisting others during examinations.

Penalty:

Examination paper cancelled.
Marks awarded as zero.
Parent meeting.
Written disciplinary record.
CIS-S008 : Plagiarism in Assignments

Rule Description:
 Submitting copied project work, homework, or assignments as one's own work.

Penalty:

Assignment rejected.
Resubmission required.
Written warning.
CIS-S009 : Use of Mobile Phone During School Hours

Rule Description:
 Using mobile phones without authorization within school premises.

Penalty:

Device confiscated.
Parent must collect device.
₹200 fine for repeated violations.
CIS-S010 : Damage to School Property

Rule Description:
 Damaging desks, benches, computers, laboratory equipment, sports equipment, library books, buses, or any school-owned property.

Penalty:

Repair/replacement cost paid by student.
Fine of ₹500.
Parent meeting.
Suspension for severe damage cases.
CIS-S011 : Library Book Non-Return

Rule Description:
 Failure to return borrowed library books beyond due date.

Penalty:

₹10 per day late fee.
Borrowing privileges suspended until return.
CIS-S012 : False Complaint or False Accusation

Rule Description:
 Knowingly making false allegations against another student or staff member.

Penalty:

Written warning.
Parent meeting.
Suspension in serious cases.
CIS-S013 : Unauthorized Access to School Systems

Rule Description:
 Attempting to access restricted school computers, accounts, Wi-Fi networks, or digital services.

Penalty:

Immediate account suspension.
Parent meeting.
Possible suspension from school.
CIS-S014 : Possession of Dangerous Items

Rule Description:
 Carrying prohibited or potentially dangerous items onto school premises.

Penalty:

Immediate confiscation.
Parent notification.
Discipline committee review.
Possible expulsion for serious violations.
CIS-S015 : Absenteeism Without Valid Reason

Rule Description:
 Missing classes without parent or guardian authorization.

Penalty:

Written warning.
Parent notification.
Attendance review.
